# Welcome

This site documents my master's thesis on the intersection of **quantum
computing** and **homomorphic encryption** (HE). It collects notebooks,
short tutorials, and background notes so anyone curious about the topic
can follow along.

## What you'll find here

- A gentle intro to quantum computing: qubits, gates, and measurement.
- A gentle intro to homomorphic encryption: what it means to compute on
  ciphertexts, and why lattice problems make it possible.
- Executable Jupyter notebooks demonstrating both — you can read them
  here, or download the `.ipynb` and run them locally.

## How to read this site

Use the sidebar on the left to navigate chapters. Notebooks are rendered
with their outputs; click the **rocket icon** at the top right of any
notebook to launch it in Binder or Colab.
